Capabilities

What the engine is good at.

We select technology around the product's constraints, not around a fashionable stack. These are the capabilities we bring to our own ventures and to studio engagements, in the same combination.

Capability areas

C1Product strategy

We take uncertain product ideas and turn them into testable interfaces, production systems, and releases that create real learning. Strategy at Rivora is a written thesis with kill-criteria, not a slide deck.

Product thesisMarket framingValidation designRoadmaps

C2Product design

Design systems, flows, and interfaces built to survive contact with engineering. Locked token foundations, dark-mode-first where the product calls for it, and prototypes honest enough to test a thesis.

Design systemsInterface prototypesInteraction designDesign tokens

C3Mobile engineering

Mobile is our home platform, not an export target. Native Android with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, iOS with SwiftUI, and Kotlin Multiplatform where one codebase genuinely serves both. Snook A Look ships this way today.

Kotlin / ComposeSwiftUIKotlin MultiplatformStore releases

C4Web platforms

The machinery real products run on: booking systems, operator dashboards, marketplaces, and admin platforms with real-time state, payments, and background workers behind them.

TypeScript / NodeNext.jsPostgreSQL / PrismaReal-time systems

C5AI-enabled experiences

AI where it changes the product, not where it decorates the pitch. We also run agentic engineering internally every day, so we know exactly what current models can and cannot carry in production.

LLM product featuresAgentic systemsEvaluation and guardrails

C6Enterprise systems and modernisation

Enterprise-informed engineering from the founder's background in large-scale environments: audit trails, access control, compliance-aware flows, and modernising platforms without stopping the business.

Architecture reviewPlatform modernisationSecurity-aware design

Engagement shape
