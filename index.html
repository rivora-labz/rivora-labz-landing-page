02 · Flagship venture

From product thesis to the App Store.

Live · Dubai

Snook A Look

A two-sided marketplace connecting cue-sports players with clubs across Dubai. Live table availability, instant booking, skill-matched opponents, and a mechanic players actually talk about: loser pays the table.

  • Players see open tables in real time and book in 30-minute slots
  • Quick Match pairs opponents by skill tier and distance
  • Clubs manage demand, tables, and reservations from one system

05 · How we build

From hypothesis to release, on rails.

Six stages, each with a clear exit condition. The same rails carry our own ventures and partner products.

01

Frame

Write the product thesis down: the user, the problem, the bet, and what would prove it wrong.

02

Prototype

Interfaces you can tap within days. Cheap to change, honest enough to test the thesis.

03

Engineer

Production architecture chosen from the product's constraints, not from a fashionable stack.

04

Validate

Testing, review gates, and real-device checks before anything reaches a user.

05

Release

Ship deliberately: staged rollouts, store submissions, monitoring from day one.

06

Evolve

Measure what happened after release and feed it back into the thesis. Every release should create learning.
