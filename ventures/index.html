Flagship · Consumer marketplace

Live · Dubai

Snook A Look

The problem: cue-sports players in Dubai drive to clubs hoping a table is free, wait with no visibility, and play the same people every week. Clubs, meanwhile, run prime tables empty on quiet nights.

The thesis: make tables and opponents visible in real time, let players book in 30-minute slots, and give clubs a demand system instead of a phone that may or may not be answered.

Live on iOS and Android, with a club-operator platform behind it.

Voice AI · Bank collections

Live demo · Award winner

Mizan

A collections call the bank can prove.

Banks chase early arrears with scripted call centres that miss the customer's language and leave a thin record of what was agreed. Mizan is a governed outbound voice agent, built with Team Mizan on ElevenLabs Agents.

  • Won the Dubai AI Voice Challenge at the Dubai AI Festival, 27 October 2026
  • Speaks Emirati Arabic, Emirati English and Indian English, and verifies identity before any debt is mentioned
  • Governed: offers only the payment plans the bank's decision engine approved, and logs every commitment
  • Bank console: live calls, validator events, follow-ups and the signed wording in force

Brand Take

Find the need. Brief from proof.

  • Drafts: a test ad with an AI presenter, ready for the team to judge

Networking

Live

We Just Met

They speak. You take a selfie. The follow-up is already in their chat.

After a good conversation at an event, the contact ends up as a crumpled card or a name you half remember. We Just Met needs nothing from the other person. Built at the Collabute x TheBlock hackathon in Dubai, August 2026.

  • Capture: they say their name, number and company out loud while the orb listens
  • Enrich: about thirty seconds later the contact is saved and enriched
  • Follow up: a composed WhatsApp message with the selfie lands in their chat
  • Zero install: the other person installs nothing; Android build and live dashboard

AI governance

Live demo

Sijil

Proof of what your AI did, on infrastructure you own.

Governments and regulated firms want to run AI on servers they own, but cannot prove afterwards what a model was asked and what it answered. Sijil sits in front of the model and keeps the receipts.

  • Policy proxy: allows, flags or blocks every model call against the policy in force
  • Sealed: each call becomes a signed, hash-chained record of model, place, policy and approver
  • Verified: an independent verifier proves the chain intact, or names the exact record that broke
  • Live demo: an insider forges record 17 outside the app; the verifier catches it
