Privacy policy.

The short version: this website stores nothing about you. The long version is below, and it is still short.

Last updated: 11 July 2026 · Applies to rivoralabz.com

Who we are

This website is operated by Rivora Labz FZE LLC, a company registered in the United Arab Emirates ("Rivora", "we"). You can reach us at hi@rivoralabz.com.

What this site collects

Nothing, by design.

  • No analytics or tracking scripts run on this site.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • The contact form does not submit data to our servers. It composes an email inside your own mail application; the message travels as ordinary email from you to us.

Third-party resources

Pages load web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser requests those files, Google receives your IP address as part of the standard request. See Google's privacy policy. The site is served through Cloudflare's network, which processes requests to deliver the pages; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Email you send us

If you email us, we keep the correspondence for as long as the conversation is useful, use it only to reply and follow up, and never sell or share it for marketing. Ask us to delete a thread and we will.

Our products

Rivora ventures such as Snook A Look are separate products with their own privacy policies, available within those products and on their own websites. This policy covers rivoralabz.com only.

Your rights

Under UAE data protection law (PDPL) and equivalent regimes, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Since this site holds none, that will usually mean email correspondence. Write to hi@rivoralabz.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with an updated date.
