About

A lab with a bias for shipping.

Rivora Labz FZE LLC is a UAE technology company built around one conviction: the distance between a good product idea and working software is where most ventures die, and closing that distance is a craft worth building a company on.

01 · The company

Umbrella by design.

What Rivora is

A product engine

An umbrella for digital ventures, technical experiments, and selective client partnerships. We create our own products, develop reusable product infrastructure, and take on a small number of engagements where our engine gives a partner an unfair advantage.

What Rivora is not

Not an agency, not a bench

We do not sell hours, staff teams, or take every brief. The company is small, focused, and technically capable by intent: founder-led, with specialist collaborators and an internal agent fleet multiplying what one determined team can carry.

02 · Founder

Narayan Dhingra

Founder and CEO. A product engineer before anything else: Android and Kotlin at the core, with enterprise engineering experience and the scars of shipping real products to real stores.

Narayan started Rivora after watching the same pattern repeat: strong ideas dying in the gap between strategy and software, and agencies billing through that gap instead of closing it. Rivora is the machine he wanted to exist: founder urgency at the front, enterprise discipline underneath, and products as the only output that counts.

Based in the UAE. Building for anywhere.

03 · Direction

Where this is going.

The long-term shape of Rivora is a portfolio: ventures that compound, infrastructure that gets reused, and a studio practice that keeps the engine sharp.

  • More ventures, same honestyThe pipeline grows one earned status at a time. Nothing gets called live until it is.
  • Infrastructure as an assetThe Fleet, the design foundations, the release rails: every venture makes the next one cheaper and faster to build.
  • UAE-born, globally mindedProducts engineered for this region's realities first, built to standards that travel anywhere.
